2D Red Ball

Shreesha Krishna

Navigating your way through numerous obstacles and scoring as you move past them.

 Your objective is to dodge incoming obstacles, represented by blue balls, while collecting points to achieve the highest score possible. The intuitive controls allow you to move the red ball up, down, left, or right, offering a seamless and engaging experience. Developed using Unity and C#, this game combines smooth gameplay mechanics with captivating visuals, ensuring an immersive adventure for players of all ages.

**How does the game actually play? (
As players use controls to move the ball in a way that it doesn't collide with others, they score points.

The speed and thrill of the game goes on as the player strikes more, the immersive animations keep the players engaged.
